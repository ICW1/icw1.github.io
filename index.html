University of Birmingham

BSc Mathematics

I earned a first class honours degree in Mathematics at Birmingham. The rigour of a pure and applied maths training — proof, probability, and mathematical modelling — became the foundation for everything I've built since: I learned to reason about systems precisely before ever writing production code.

University of New Mexico

MSc Applied Mathematics

I moved to Albuquerque, USA to study for an MSc in Applied Mathematics, graduating with a 3.99 GPA. My thesis applied control theory to the double pendulum inverted on a cart — a classic hard problem in non-linear dynamics — implemented in MATLAB. Away from the maths department, I raced cross country and track for the New Mexico Lobos — living and training at altitude in the American Southwest took my running to another level.

Sky

Data Scientist · 2019 – 2021

I started my industry career at Sky, applying data science across the business: survival and time-series analysis for broadband churn, statistically valid A/B testing to guide technology decisions, and process control for KPI monitoring. I built production environments in GCP with BigQuery and led data science workshops for analyst teams — my first taste of teaching technical practice at scale.

Ipsos

Senior Data Scientist · 2021 – 2022

At Ipsos I worked on the IRIS Project, the UK's online audience measurement solution. I orchestrated complex data fusions using XGBoost to link millions of devices based on browsing behaviour, implemented anomaly detection to filter unnatural panellist behaviour, and built automated reporting and parameterised Argo/Kubernetes workflows on GCP.

MindGym

Data Scientist · 2022 – 2023

At MindGym, a behavioural science and coaching company, I created and shipped a virtual coach: a ReAct chatbot agent with retrieval-augmented generation, persistent memory, and tool use — built with LangChain in 2022, before "agents" were a buzzword. It was deployed at scale via Slack and launched as a product.

I also built the company's data platform, centralising multiple sources into an S3 data lake with Argo workflows on Kubernetes, and collaborated with behavioural scientists on NLP analysis of wellbeing survey data.

From my blog post on building LangChain chatbots with memory.

Travtus

Senior NLP Data Scientist · 2023 – 2024

At Travtus, an AI property management startup, I led the migration from a RASA intent/response framework to a LangChain agent with RAG, chat memory, and fine-tuning — pioneering a process-retrieval approach where the agent selects and plans execution steps from tenant input. I built the evaluation suites and benchmark datasets that made model selection a data-driven decision rather than a matter of taste.

Connect Earth

NLP / LLM Data Science Lead · 2024 – 2025

At Connect Earth, a carbon accounting fintech, I designed and built an AI-driven carbon categorisation system for banking transactions from scratch — and shipped it to millions of banking customers across the UK and Europe.

I fine-tuned open-source LLMs (DistilBERT, Mistral-7B, LLaMA) with quantised LoRA adapters, cutting training time by ~60% and inference latency by over 40% — enabling deployment on small serverless CPU instances. The production pipeline on AWS Lambda, SQS, and SageMaker handled hundreds of thousands of monthly API calls at over 90% precision.

I also built the Carbon Copilot: a multi-modal LLM agent (LangGraph + Bedrock + Claude) for emissions reporting and regulatory document Q&A, reducing client reporting time from five-plus minutes to under ten seconds.

Elsewhen

Senior AI Engineer · 2025 – Present

I now consult to leading global enterprises through Elsewhen, a London technology consultancy building AI-driven products. I designed and delivered a multi-index semantic search system for a leading global travel company — vector embeddings combined with structured filters in an OpenSearch query layer, across multi-account AWS infrastructure — now live in production. Beyond delivery, I shape the work itself: converting ambiguous, solution-oriented requirements into structured technical roadmaps, and authoring the AI expertise and feasibility sections of major public sector proposals.

Running
